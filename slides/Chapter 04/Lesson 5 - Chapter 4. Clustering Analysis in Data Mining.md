​Because it discretizes the data space through a grid structure, and ​estimates the density by counting the number of points in a grid cell. ​Why it is density-based? ​Because a cluster actually is maximal set of connected dense units in a subspace. ​That means a unit is dense in the fraction of the total ​data points contained in the units, it exceeds certain parameters. ​Then you try to connect those dense units into a structure, into a cluster. ​That means it is density-based. ​But why it is subspace clustering?
